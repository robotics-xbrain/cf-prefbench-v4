# Usage terms

This anonymous artifact is released **for the purpose of double-blind peer review
and research reproduction** of the accompanying paper.

- **Code** (`cf_pref_learning/`, `scripts/`): research use; intended to be released
  under a permissive open-source license (e.g. MIT/Apache-2.0) upon de-anonymization.
- **CF-PrefBench v4 data** (`data/`): synthetic, deterministically rendered by a
  simulator; no human subjects, no personal data, no scraped content. Intended for a
  CC-BY-style license upon de-anonymization.
- **Trained LIB checkpoints** (`checkpoints/`): research use only.
- **Third-party pretrained encoders** (OpenAI CLIP, OpenCLIP-LAION-2B, SigLIP-WebLI):
  **not** included; governed by their own upstream licenses. Download via the
  respective libraries.
- **Final paper PDF** (`docs/EMNLP_final.pdf`): the anonymous submission, for reviewer
  reference.

No warranty. During the review period, do not attempt to de-anonymize the authors.
A formal license will be attached to the public release.
